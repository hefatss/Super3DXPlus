# Changelog

What Super3DX+ adds or changes compared with [Re3DX+](NOTICE.md). Longer explanations are in `docs/`.

## Input and menus

- Controller-first menus: full pad and keyboard control on every screen, with rebindable keys, pad buttons and analog axes
- Controller input filtering, deadzone, response and button-steering speed tuning
- Controller vibration (wall hits, expansion joints, tyre slip) with adjustable strength
- TOUCH LAYOUT (the touch controls' screen, formerly KEY LAYOUT, issue 18): besides the three groups of touch keys, the race's gauges can be dragged and resized: the dials, the speed plate, the gear shifter, the rank and distance, the time left, the map and the rival info (50% to 200%, about their own middle; a console left alone stays exactly where it was). The arcade-style HUD is untouched.
- CAMERA SHAKE (in the game settings): smooths out the view's shake at speed and over rough road, 0% to 100%
- TOUCH LAYOUT: the shift up and shift down keys are now groups of their own, sized and placed apart from the pedals and from each other (a layout saved before keeps them where they were)
- Android: coloured static on some road stretches (Nagoya's first reflection cube has one face of another size, which Vulkan read past its edge; issue 24)
- NETWORK OPTIONS shows how the last host or join attempt went (no answer, version mismatch, host closed...) instead of "not connected"; the same line goes to the log
- Windows: the game asks laptops with two graphics cards to use the fast one (NVIDIA Optimus and AMD switchable graphics; Windows' Graphics settings can still override it)
- Steering wheel support (untested on hardware): wheel axes and buttons are bindable, and force feedback (steering pull, centering, damping, road effects) through SDL haptic, with a WHEEL list in KEY BINDINGS for the device and gains
- Rebuilt main menu flow: PLAY / SETTINGS / EXIT, then car selection, then Solo / Versus / Car settings
- Stock, Full Tune and Hell's Tuning starting points (Hell's Tuning begins the story on Ch. 101)
- Restored AT / MT transmission select screen
- Special color selection for cars
- Restored suspension lowering for tuned cars
- Volume mixer
- GAME OPTIONS has a GRAPHICS sub-menu now: textures, reflection, HD textures, wet road, anti-aliasing, resolution, window mode and DISPLAY OPTIONS moved into it
- Versus: the second race of a session could stand still at its start on both machines for ever (the clock comparison kept the last race's numbers, so the client thought it was thousands of frames ahead of the host and dropped every step, and a machine that takes no step sends nothing); it starts over with each race now
- SMOOTH MOTION (GAME OPTIONS > GRAPHICS, on by default): on a display that is not 60 Hz (90, 120, 144 Hz) and in versus races the physics steps run on the clock's time and the car, camera, tyres, rivals and traffic are drawn blended between the last two steps, so motion is even at any refresh rate; a versus client follows the host's clock by running a little fast or slow (up to 3 %) in place of dropping or doubling a step. Costs one frame (16 ms) of delay; menus, HUD and effects are unchanged. `MT3DX_INTERP=0|1` forces it for a test
- Android: a 60 FPS LOCK (GAME OPTIONS > GRAPHICS) for phones with a 90 or 120 Hz panel (issue 32): it asks the system for 60 Hz and, on a panel that stays at a whole multiple of 60 (120 Hz), draws one frame every second refresh, each on its own game step, instead of two frames per step
- DISPLAY OPTIONS on PC (GRAPHICS), with a BLACK LEVEL setting and a bar of greys to judge it by: pulls the blacks down for displays or drivers that show the picture grey and washed out (a limited range signal on a full range monitor, Auto HDR, colour enhancement); 50% undoes a 16-235 limited range
- Option to skip the intro cutscenes, and restored missing intro cutscenes
- Information screen

## Game modes

- Fixed versus play over LAN or online, with restored route choice at junctions, last-stretch calls, placings and disconnect handling
- Versus: each rival's ping (P: 21, green to 75 ms, yellow to 120, orange to 200, red over) under its ADVANTAGE; the winning track's name on the HP screen's band; the host's area result reaches a machine that missed it; the interrupt switch's headlight flash restored (HEADLIGHT FLASH in KEY BINDINGS, L / pad X); the junction flare covers a wide window; the LEAVE IT TO THE OTHERS key is gone
- The mouse pointer hides in a race (PC)
- NETWORK OPTIONS in the game options, for versus connection trouble: ALLOW THROUGH FIREWALL (Windows: asks Windows again, with an administrator prompt, after a No on its first question), OPEN LOG FOLDER, COPY DIAGNOSTICS (version, address, hosting, ping and the last race's lost packets to the clipboard), PING ON THE HUD, DETAILED NET LOG (on by default), and the connection's live numbers
- The typed or saved server address is forgotten when the game closes
- Logs are scrubbed of public IP addresses, room codes and user names in paths (Windows at start and exit, Android at start), so they can be posted on GitHub
- Versus: players click READY before each round (the host's START waits for them; AUTO READY in the lobby brings the old way back); a race that stands still for 30 s sends everyone back to try again; BLACK LEVEL is saved now
- The music select starts on RANDOM every time (versus and single player), not on the last race's song
- Music selection by the host and by each race's winner in versus
- Time attack rankings with car class and level, kept across sessions
- Restored 10 Outrun's special OST and in-game functionality
- Music track and time zone selection in 10 Outrun
- 10 Outrun rival line animation and rival marker
- Restored junction-choice walls and the "Choose a direction" animation

## Graphics and HUD

- Fixed many missing animations (rival markers, etc.)
- Restored original reflection handling, applied to rival cars
- Tire smoke, headlight flares and the missing shift light
- Fixed the reflective buildings of the morning Tokyo maps (New Belt Line, C1, Yokohane) showing checkered, see-through facades with things moving behind them: the reflection program added the gloss map's alpha to the base alpha, which went above 1 and, blended into a floating-point target, subtracted what was behind. The alpha is clamped; both map vertex shaders also declare the vertex position invariant, so surfaces at nearly the same depth keep their order on every GPU
- Fixed a solid white wall at the exit of the C1 tunnels (about 10.8 km into C1 inward): the data build dropped the alpha of the tunnel mouths' faint white light mask (nondxt_sk_nk_lighteff01, alpha 17 at most) as if it were a sky dome, so it drew opaque. Rebuild the Tokyo packs (python tools/pipeline.py convert <arcade data> --only tokyo-day) to get the fix
- The whole game can be built on an Android device with no PC (data and app, in Termux): `docs/ANDROID_ON_DEVICE.md`. `build_apk.py` uses Termux's own tools there; the converters find Termux's font packages; the Khronos EGL headers are bundled
- Windows: a racing wheel on its maker's driver (a Fanatec) says "forces not available" and its axes and buttons cannot be bound: SDL's RawInput and Windows.Gaming.Input drivers took it first and have no forces. The wheel now comes through DirectInput; the reason SDL gives for a refused force device is shown in the settings (issue 2)
- Setup checks GitHub for a newer version when it starts and offers to install it (git pull in a checkout; otherwise the changed files are downloaded and replaced, the old ones kept in build/update-backup), then does the quick player rebuild or the data conversion as needed; `setup.bat update` only checks; it remembers the game folder of the last build
- Windows: the log now says what the driver's default framebuffer is (sRGB capable, `GL_FRAMEBUFFER_SRGB`), and the game switches the sRGB encoding off if the driver has it on: a try at the washed-out colours on AMD graphics cards (issue 15), where the bundled ANGLE (`--angle`) shows correct colours. Where it was on, nothing changed visibly on an Nvidia card
- Smooth motion: the blended camera rotation is kept a true rotation (it ran a hair short between two steps, and the picture pulsed with the blend, worst in tight bends and in the first-person view)
- Smooth motion: the first-person view is held to the car's body (the eye leaves it only over about half a second), as the road's jolts on the arcade's camera showed as a wobble at 120 Hz; with smooth motion off the view is as it was, and the CAMERA SHAKE option still works (and the filter no longer gives up on a jolt of more than a few units)
- Japan build: the manual gearbox's "2速に入れてください" at the start has its digit at the front again, in the arcade's own place (issue 38: the Export fix put it after the text, over the Japanese characters)
- Linux and macOS: the conversion's view of the dump is made in the system's temporary folder when `build/` is on a drive that cannot hold symbolic links (an external exFAT/NTFS drive; issue 39), and a failed link says which folders it was, whether they exist and what to do (`SUPER3DX_VIEW_DIR`)
- Linux and macOS: `setup-android.sh` and `setup-pi.sh` are executable now (issue 37: `./setup-android.sh` said the file was not an executable; `sh setup-android.sh` always worked)
- Versus: the "Keep going!" call in the last 700 m is shown only to the car right behind the leader; the leader still gets "Don't get caught!"
- Windows: on ANGLE a 120 Hz screen shows every refresh (ANGLE's swap interval 2 gave 37 fps), so smooth motion is on in races there whatever its option says

- Windows: AMD graphics cards now start on the bundled ANGLE (OpenGL ES over Direct3D 11) in place of AMD's own OpenGL ES, which drew every other frame of a race from a stale buffer on a Radeon RX 9070 XT (issue 33) and lifted the blacks on others (issue 15); `--native-gl`, `MT3DX_NATIVE_GL=1`, a `native-gl.txt` beside the exe or a build with `WMMT_NO_AUTO_ANGLE=1` keeps the driver's

- Windows: a PC whose graphics driver has no OpenGL ES (a virtual machine, remote desktop, an old or generic driver: "Could not initialize OpenGL / GLES library", issue 20) now runs on the bundled ANGLE (`third_party/angle`, OpenGL ES over Direct3D 11); if that fails too, a message says what is wrong instead of the game just closing
- The turbo's whine was far too loud against the engine ("a jet engine", issue 6): measured on a recording of the arcade's own tuned Evo IX it sits about 19 dB under the engine, here it sat 9 to 11 dB under. Its level is now the arcade's; the whine's pitch was already right

## Platforms and builds

- Export (English) build support and an English language layer
- Raspberry Pi 5 support with composite CRT output (and optional HDMI)
- Windows, Linux and updated Android players
- Windowed / fullscreen mode (F11 or Alt+Enter), remembered between starts
- `build.py` and `tools/pipeline.py` build driver: Python 3.9+ and a C compiler, no WSL
- Guided setup (`setup.bat` / `setup.sh`): installs what it needs, finds and checks your game folder, builds, and explains failures in plain language
- Updates only redo what changed: a new version usually rebuilds just the game program in minutes, keeping your data and saves
- `setup-pi`: builds and installs the game on a Raspberry Pi over the network, with the boot service and composite/HDMI output
- `setup-android`: builds one self-contained Android app (any OS) and can install it over USB
